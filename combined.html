Grooming with a city standard

Gulu City Barbershop started with one simple frustration: good cuts were hard to find and impossible to book. So we built a shop where the blade work is precise, the tools are sanitised in front of you, and a cape is always clean. Whether you want a classic taper or the freshest trend in town, you leave looking like the best version of yourself — and the nail parlour opposite means the whole family gets sorted in one trip.
